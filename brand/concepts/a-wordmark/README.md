# Concept A: Wordmark-led (QuotesDone Technologies)

1. **Palette:** Ink `#17130F` (warm near-black, primary), Ember `#F0502A` (single accent: only the Q's cut), Bone `#F3EEE4` (text on dark / paper). Warm and dune-and-ember, deliberately away from blue SaaS; no teal, gold or navy, so no clash with any product brand.
2. **Typography:** `QuotesDone` set in Geist (OFL) at weight 640, tracking -14, outlined; `TECHNOLOGIES` Geist 520 caps, letter-spaced to justify exactly to the wordmark width. All text is outlined paths.
3. **The ownable detail:** a custom Q: a ring opened on the lower right by a 45-degree cut, with a detached Ember slash passing through it (a quote struck through to "done"). The symbol is that Q alone; it is the only drawn letter and is generic enough for every product under the group.
4. **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least one Q-ring stroke x 2 (about the height of the "u" counter, 0.25 x cap height) on all sides; minimum width 120px for the lockup, 16px for the symbol.
5. **Files:** `mark.svg` (light bg), `mark-dark.svg`, `mark-onecolour-black.svg`, `logo-horizontal-light.svg`, `logo-horizontal-dark.svg`, `logo-onecolour-black.svg`; one-colour versions drop Ember and rely on the gap.
