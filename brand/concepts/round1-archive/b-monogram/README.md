# QuotesDone Technologies - Concept B, Abstract monogram
Palette: Ink Green #0F1F1C (structure, trust, near-black with a green cast, not another blue), Signal Coral #FF5B3A (the "done" block, warm and awake against ink), Bone #F4F0E8 (mark and text on dark). Neutrals: #5E6B67 / #9AA8A3 for the descriptor.
Idea: a circle is the Q, the D is cut out of it as negative space, and a coral square sits at the tail position as the "done" stamp. One memorable move, three shapes.
Type: wordmark is Geist SemiBold (600, -1.2% tracking), TECHNOLOGIES is Geist Medium in caps, tracked to match the wordmark width; all text is outlined (SVG-only, no font needed).
Clear space: keep one coral-square width (about 22% of mark height) free on all sides; minimum mark size 16 px, minimum lockup width 120 px; on dark use the Bone mark, never Ink.
One-colour: swap every fill to a single colour; the 14-unit gap around the square keeps the Q readable without the coral.
