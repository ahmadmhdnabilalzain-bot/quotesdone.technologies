## 1. Voice and rules

1. We sound like the products: short, plain, sure. A headline sentence runs 2 to 6 words and ends with a full stop.
2. One claim per line, then one fact that earns it. Numbers replace adjectives.
3. Products speak first. The company is the name at the bottom of each one.
4. "We" is the company, "you" is the reader, and each product uses its own name.
5. We state only what a live site or a repo shows. Status is written as it stands: live, coming soon, in development.
6. Never: revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless, unlock, empower, leverage, elevate, transform, magic, journey, effortless, perfect, ideal, world-class, innovative, solutions.
7. Never: first, only, best, leading, trusted by, thousands of, award-winning, compliant, certified, or any customer, user count, partner or funding.
8. Never "AI" as a selling word (QuotesDone says "smart engine"; Sonobolt bans it). No exclamation marks, no em dashes, no rows of three adjectives.
9. Legal name is "QuotesDone Technologies", a Sole Establishment. Never "L.L.C.", "LLC", "Group" or "Holdings".
10. British spelling ("licence"), except the licence's printed title "Dubai Trader License".

**Headline:** Open one. Leave the rest sealed.

## 8. Arabic readiness

Write these fresh in Arabic, with a native copywriter. Do not translate them.

1. **Hero, "Software, built and run in Dubai."** "Run" has no single Arabic verb that means both operating a product and managing it. The line needs a new form.
2. **Sub-line.** "Runs them after launch" carries the same problem.
3. **The four statement headlines,** especially "An estimate is called an estimate." and "Your details are not passed around.", which rest on English idiom.
4. **Principle headlines,** "The price comes first.", "Keep less." and "Say what we do not know." "Keep less." has no natural two-word Arabic form.
5. **Product headlines.** "Open one. Leave the rest sealed." should come from QuotesDone's existing Arabic page if one exists. "Your songs. One mix." should come from Sonobolt's Arabic writer, since "mix" has no settled word (Sonobolt VOICE.md). "Payroll that shows its working." rests on an English school idiom.
6. **The 404 line,** "The products do." The joke depends on English ellipsis.
7. **About headline,** "A Dubai company that ships." "Ships" is software slang.

Decisions for the owner before Arabic is written:
- **Register.** QuotesDone uses formal Modern Standard Arabic with the formal plural. Sonobolt plans a lightly Gulf-leaning standard for marketing. The hub needs one of the two. `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]`
- **Trade name.** Use كوتس دن للتكنولوجيا, exactly as printed on sonobolt.com and in the licence record (`company_identity_sources.json`).
- **Licence line.** Take the Arabic from the licence itself. Sonobolt's Arabic footer says رخصة تاجر (مهنية), which follows its English wording, not quotesdone.com's.
- **Kept in Latin script:** QuotesDone, Sonobolt, MubinFlow, DJ, BPM, SIF, AED. The logos do not mirror on right-to-left pages.
- **Numerals** (Western or Arabic-Indic) and how money is written ("AED 99" or "99 درهم") are decided once, for the whole hub.
- **Legal and licence text** goes to a qualified legal translator, not a copywriter.
